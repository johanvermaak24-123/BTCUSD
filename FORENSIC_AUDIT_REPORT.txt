REAL DEFECTS FOUND AND FIXED DURING THIS AUDIT
1. Higher-timeframe aggregation could include the current incomplete bucket.
   FIX: only fully closed source/aggregate candles can drive closed-timeframe logic.

2. Learning resolution could use an open 5-minute bar in some circumstances.
   FIX: outcome learning resolves only from closed BTC 5-minute OHLC bars.

3. Cross-market DXY evidence could become stale, especially while traditional markets are closed.
   FIX: DXY and ETH confirmation now require freshness; stale evidence is ignored.

4. Cost geometry was initially validated before final price rounding.
   FIX: spread/cost geometry is revalidated on the actual rounded order prices.

5. Adaptive Entry 1/2/3 selection could choose Entry 1 even when that rung's TP1 distance was too small versus spread.
   FIX: each candidate rung must individually pass cost geometry before it may become the active entry. If E1 is unsafe, the selector falls back to an eligible rung (normally E2).
