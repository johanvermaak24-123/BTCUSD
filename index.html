Important: probabilistic research software. BTC R6.11.3 uses the Pepperstone BTCUSD symbol profile supplied from MT5: floating spread, 2 digits, contract size 1 BTC, minimum volume 0.01 lot, volume step 0.01, market execution, and bid-price charting. The $15 value is retained only as a conservative execution-cost reference from an observed Pepperstone quote; the model remains floating and can increase its execution cost when the live feed widens. Entry triggering is side-aware (BUY uses ask where available; SELL uses bid), while open-position exit/TP/SL tracking is side-aware (BUY closes on bid; SELL closes on ask, approximated from bid bars plus execution spread when exact ask history is unavailable). Quote staleness/market-state guards block fresh entries during suspected maintenance or feed interruption without pretending to know the broker-server timezone. The public/MT5-sourced BTC basis remains consistent for prediction and learning; broker tolerance is an execution uncertainty buffer, not a fake Pepperstone price correction. Exact MT5 fills should still be entered after a real trade. Public derivatives and order-flow feeds are exchange-level BTC perpetual proxies and may differ from Pepperstone BTCUSD; Bybit/Binance depth, taker flow and Bybit liquidation pressure are bounded evidence only, automatically down/up-weighted by observed outcomes, and never replace the broker execution feed. Missing microstructure is a fallback condition, not a hard entry veto. The fast learner uses only fresh live-price checkpoints after a separate 15-minute window; it does not inflate the strict 1H/3H/6H proof counters. No profit or predictive-accuracy guarantee is implied.
